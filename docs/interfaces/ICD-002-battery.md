# OR-ICD-002: Battery Interface

**Revision:** 0.1  
**Status:** Concept

## Required Behaviors

The interface shall:

- permit tool-free battery replacement;
- prevent reverse insertion or reverse electrical connection;
- prevent exposed contacts from creating a credible short-circuit hazard;
- retain the battery during running, impacts, and routine drops;
- support safe insertion and removal while the system is off;
- preserve settings across battery replacement;
- provide enough information for safe charge and discharge control;
- isolate a failed battery from the remainder of the system where practical.

## Deferred Decisions

- cell chemistry and format;
- pack voltage;
- smart versus passive pack;
- sealing method;
- latch architecture;
- charging inside or outside the wearable.
