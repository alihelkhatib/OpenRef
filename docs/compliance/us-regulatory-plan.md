# United States Regulatory and Compliance Plan

**Document ID:** OR-CMP-001  
**Revision:** 0.1  
**Status:** Preliminary

## Market Assumption

Version 1 is intended for the United States. Pennsylvania is the initial development and field-test location.

## Radio Authorization

OpenRef is expected to contain at least one intentional radiator and digital circuitry. The final authorization path depends on the selected radio architecture and whether a certified radio module is used.

The preliminary plan is:

1. Prefer operation under FCC Part 15 rules that do not require individual user licensing.
2. Evaluate certified radio modules where they materially reduce schedule and compliance risk.
3. Preserve module grant conditions, approved antennas, layout guidance, shielding, and integration instructions.
4. Plan for final-product testing of applicable unintentional-radiator, intentional-radiator, and host integration requirements.
5. Do not assume that use of a certified module removes all final-product obligations.
6. Establish a U.S. responsible party before commercial distribution.
7. Develop required labels, user notices, technical records, and production change controls before release.

Candidate spectrum under 47 CFR 15.247 includes 902–928 MHz and 2400–2483.5 MHz, but no band is selected by this document.

## Antenna Constraint

The architecture shall account for FCC antenna restrictions applicable to intentional radiators. Serviceability goals shall not create a user-accessible general-purpose antenna connector unless expressly permitted by the selected authorization path.

## Battery and Product Safety

The product shall use recognized battery safety practices, qualified cells or packs, protected charging, fault containment, and controlled transportation documentation.

Commercial release planning shall evaluate, as applicable:

- battery cell and pack safety certification;
- UN 38.3 transportation testing and documentation;
- charger or external power-supply safety;
- product electrical safety;
- material and labeling obligations;
- workplace controls for assembly, storage, service, and damaged batteries.

## Accessibility and Consumer Documentation

Instructions shall communicate safe battery handling, charging limits, environmental limitations, disposal, service boundaries, and radio compliance notices in clear U.S. English.

## Pennsylvania

No Pennsylvania-specific radio authorization is currently assumed. State and local requirements may affect business registration, sales, environmental disposal, workplace practices, and field-test permissions, but not the baseline FCC radio architecture.

## Release Gate

No hardware may be represented as compliant or marketed commercially until the responsible party has obtained and retained the required authorization evidence.
