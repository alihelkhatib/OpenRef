# Operating Environment

OpenRef shall operate reliably:

- Outdoors
- Indoors
- In rain
- In snow
- In direct sunlight
- During day and night matches
- Between approximately -10°C and +45°C
- In environments containing Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and other RF interference
- On grass
- On artificial turf
- On dirt and gravel
- While officials are running, sprinting, jumping, and making rapid directional changes

The system shall support both outdoor and indoor sports.
