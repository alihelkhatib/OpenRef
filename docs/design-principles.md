# OpenRef Design Principles

## The official should never have to think about the equipment.

Communication equipment exists to disappear into the background.

Officials should focus entirely on the match, not on operating, troubleshooting, or managing their communication system.

Every design decision should reduce cognitive load during officiating.

When choosing between two solutions, prefer the one that requires less thought from the official while maintaining reliability.

## Technology should disappear.

The best communication system is the one officials never have to think about.

Complex engineering should remain hidden behind an intuitive, dependable experience. Every interaction should feel deliberate, refined, and effortless.
