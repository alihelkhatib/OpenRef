# System Quality Attributes

**Document ID:** OR-ARC-002  
**Revision:** 0.1  
**Status:** Draft

## Reliability

The system shall preserve useful crew communication despite a single wearable-unit failure where technically feasible. Recovery behavior shall be automatic for transient faults and explicit for unrecoverable faults.

## Availability

Communication availability shall be measured during representative match use, including temporary interference, movement, and body shadowing.

## Latency

End-to-end conversational latency shall be low enough to support natural interruption and urgent officiating communication. A quantitative limit remains TBD pending human-factors testing and architecture analysis.

## Intelligibility

Speech shall remain intelligible during wind, crowd noise, breathing noise, and movement representative of soccer and rugby officiating.

## Security and Privacy

Only authorized crew members shall participate in a crew session. Nearby crews shall not hear or join one another through normal operation or foreseeable user error.

## Usability

Core match operation shall not require a display, phone, menu navigation, or continuous visual attention.

## Serviceability

The battery and defined wear components shall be replaceable. Diagnostics shall distinguish major fault domains without requiring destructive disassembly.

## Environmental Robustness

The product shall tolerate rain, sweat, mud, dust, vibration, drops, storage in vehicles, and repeated transport. Quantitative environmental classes remain TBD.

## Maintainability

Interfaces, states, error codes, and configuration formats shall be documented and versioned.

## Manufacturability

Every production unit shall support deterministic programming, identity provisioning, calibration where required, and acceptance testing.
