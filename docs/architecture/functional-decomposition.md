# Functional Decomposition

**Status:** Draft v0.1

Top-level function: provide dependable, natural, private communication among an authorized officiating crew.

Primary functions:

1. Manage crew identity and membership
2. Capture speech
3. Process audio
4. Transport voice and control information
5. Route and mix audio
6. Reproduce speech
7. Present status
8. Accept controls
9. Manage power and charging
10. Protect crew and firmware integrity
11. Detect and recover from faults
12. Store configuration
13. Support diagnostics
14. Update and recover firmware
15. Monitor hardware
