# ADR-0002: Version 1 Market Scope

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-07-29

## Decision

OpenRef Version 1 will be designed initially for the United States market. Pennsylvania will serve as the initial development and field-validation environment.

## Consequences

- FCC equipment authorization governs the baseline radio compliance path.
- U.S. English is the initial controlled documentation language.
- Initial power, labeling, sales, and service assumptions are U.S.-oriented.
- CE, UKCA, and other international requirements are deferred.
- Architecture should avoid unnecessary barriers to future international variants but shall not absorb international compliance cost in Version 1 without a separate decision.
