# OpenRef Project Charter

## Vision

Advance sports officiating through open, professional-grade communication systems that are reliable, accessible, and built for the people who use them.

---

## Mission

OpenRef develops open-source communication systems for rugby and soccer match officials.

---

## Initial Users

- Rugby Referees
- Rugby Assistant Referees
- Soccer Referees
- Soccer Assistant Referees
- Fourth Officials
- Fifth Officials

Future versions may support additional sports and officiating roles.

---

## Core Problem

Officials depend on uninterrupted communication to manage matches safely and accurately.

OpenRef exists to provide communication systems that are dependable, accessible, and designed specifically for officials.

---

## Core Values

### Reliability First

Communication must remain dependable throughout every match.

### Officials Before Profit

The needs of officials come before unnecessary features or profit optimization.

### Open by Default

Hardware, software, documentation, and protocols should be as open as practical.

### Built to Last

Equipment should withstand years of professional use.

### Simple Under Pressure

Officials should never have to think about operating their equipment during a match.

---

## Scope

OpenRef develops:

- Communication hardware
- Headsets
- Audio accessories
- Push-to-talk devices
- Charging systems
- Configuration software
- Documentation
- Testing tools

Core functionality shall never depend on cloud services or subscriptions.
