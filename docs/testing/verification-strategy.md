# Verification and Validation Strategy

**Document ID:** OR-TST-001  
**Revision:** 0.1  
**Status:** Draft

## Verification Levels

1. Requirement inspection
2. Engineering analysis
3. Unit and module test
4. Subsystem bench test
5. Integrated system test
6. Environmental and abuse test
7. Representative match simulation
8. Controlled field trial
9. Production acceptance test

## Core Verification Campaigns

### Communication

- six-user simultaneous speech;
- conversational latency;
- packet or frame loss behavior;
- temporary interference recovery;
- unit loss and rejoin;
- nearby independent crews;
- malformed or unauthorized join attempts.

### Audio

- speech intelligibility;
- wind and breathing noise;
- crowd-noise rejection;
- clipping and overload;
- approved-headset variation;
- output-level safety.

### Power

- representative match endurance;
- low-battery warning accuracy;
- battery replacement;
- abnormal charging;
- cold and hot operation;
- aging and reduced-capacity behavior.

### Environmental and Mechanical

- rain and sweat;
- dust and mud contamination;
- drop;
- vibration and transport;
- cable pull and snag;
- button and connector life;
- battery retention.

### Firmware and Recovery

- interrupted update;
- corrupted image;
- watchdog recovery;
- configuration corruption;
- safe fallback;
- version compatibility.

### Human Factors

- first-time setup;
- setup under time pressure;
- operation with gloves and wet hands;
- interpretation of indications;
- fault recovery without technical assistance.

## Validation

Validation shall use experienced rugby and soccer officials in representative movement, noise, weather, and match workflows.

A requirement is not considered validated merely because it passed a laboratory test.
